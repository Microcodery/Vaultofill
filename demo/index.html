Simulated install. The real extension runs this same pipeline with an on-device LLM; this demo uses a deterministic stand-in model, so there's no ~1 GB download.
green = known (from your vault) yellow = linked (a likely match to confirm) red = new (unrecognized field)

The vault is pre-seeded, so personal fields fill green on every form. To see a new field carry across sites: fill a red field, click its tier badge to Permanent or Temporary (One-time isn't saved), Fill, then open another form that asks the same thing differently — it turns green/yellow.
